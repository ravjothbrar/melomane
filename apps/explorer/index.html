          A map of music, by how it is listened to and what it is made of

          What is this?

          Melomane is an interactive map of … tracks. Three models learn from the same tracks in the same way and differ only in what they are allowed to read. Pick any track to see its neighbours in each model, then steer the query toward a genre or an emergent direction.

          Why?

          Music recommenders are black boxes: they tell you what to play next, never why. Melomane makes that geometry inspectable, so you can see whether two tracks sit together because people play them together or because of what they are made of.

          Streaming Habits Melomane-Cooc

          Learned only from listening sessions: two tracks are close when the same people play them in the same sitting.

          Pure Sound Melomane-Composition

          Learned only from measured audio and lyric features (timbre, rhythm, harmony, loudness, lyric themes), with no listening data at all.

          The Hybrid Melomane-Core

          Listening sessions and measured features together, with listening weighted ten to one.
